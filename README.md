One image summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026, drawn by `main.py`
(Python 3.13 stdlib only: own rasterizer, hand-built 5x7 bitmap font,
zlib PNG encoder; deterministic, no network, no randomness). Entry for
the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124)
"Sum up 24 November's world news in one image", entered as @jnck1046.
This is a resubmit: v1 ranked outside the paid zone, so v2 adds six
more story panels and a longer secondary list for breadth.

Run: `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py`
→ writes `out/entry.png` (1024x1024, 33,605 bytes, byte-identical
every run).

## Layout

- Masthead: `THE WORLD AT A CROSSROADS` + gold
  `TUESDAY 24 NOVEMBER 2026`.
- Centre: Earth (continents, graticule, glow, gold trade arcs).
- 13 story panels, every label names its place:
  - Hero: ICJ finds MYANMAR guilty of genocide (Rohingya 2017,
    ruling 9-6, first state found responsible, 14 nations back it).
  - NATO vows to defend space (Russia may launch a nuclear
    satellite within 60 days); COP31 ends without a deal.
  - Bottom rows: LIBYA ship strikes (UN probe demanded, Tripoli
    wants answers), STRAIT OF HORMUZ record Iranian attacks,
    GAZA strikes kill at least six, NVIDIA's Huang is back as CEO,
    US states sue over GROK images (Oregon and Virginia sue SpaceX),
    CHAPO ISIDRO captured in Sinaloa (US drone aid), SOLOMON
    ISLANDS end their China security pact, DRC fighting traps Ebola
    responders, NEPAL M5.6 quake near the China border, US loses
    measles elimination status after 25 years.
- Footer ticker: also on 24 Nov — UK sanctions Myanmar generals,
  Germany gains Ukraine Delta access, Tiangong hosts its first
  foreign astronaut, Sanofi buys Insmed for $41B, FDA approves the
  first limb-girdle drug, most distant black hole found, wars
  threaten food aid for 7.3M more people, 47,000 women killed by
  partners in 2025, Netanyahu pushes Trump on Iran, Iraq PM takes
  over Kurd oilfields — plus the sources line.

All facts are 24 November 2026 only, as reported that day. The
Hormuz panel shows a tense strait, not burning tankers; the Libya
panel is the 24 Nov probe and Tripoli's demand, not the 22 Nov blast.
